# Weekly Money / Revenue Review Format

The weekly Commander money report must help Josh pick the next income/leverage move without reading logs or maintaining lists.

Default schedule:
- Cron job: `weekly-money-review`
- Time: Mondays at 8:00am local
- Delivery: Telegram to Josh
- Durable artifact: `WEEKLY_MONEY_REVIEW.md`

## Required output

```text
💸 WEEKLY MONEY / REVENUE REVIEW — YYYY-MM-DD → YYYY-MM-DD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 SCOREBOARD
- Top revenue lane: one lane only
- Current blocker: the one thing stopping signal/cash
- Metric/proxy: one useful proof-of-progress number
- Safety state: no posting/sending/spending/secrets/trades/account actions

✅ WHAT MOVED THIS WEEK
- 1–3 bullets max

🧠 MONEY THESIS
One sentence: where the best near-term leverage is.

🔪 KILL / SCALE / PARK
- Kill: what to stop
- Scale: what to increase
- Park: what to defer

🎯 NEXT 7 DAYS
Primary money move: exactly one.
- 15 min:
- 30 min:
- 60 min:

✅ APPROVAL NEEDED FROM JOSH
- Copy/paste phrase(s), or “none”

🧯 COMMANDER ACCOUNTABILITY CALL-OUT
One blunt call-out: too many projects, fake productivity, overbuilding, avoiding sales/marketing, health risk, risky crypto thinking, not enough fun, or wank.

🛡 SAFETY FOOTER
No posting. No sending. No spending. No secrets. No trades. No account actions. No final financial advice.
```

## Rules

- Separate from the Daily Dispatch; do not merge into one giant digest.
- Under ~80 lines.
- One primary money move, not a menu.
- Mention only useful metrics/proxies; do not invent numbers.
- If Bad Boys is blocked on Josh, name the backstop lane.
- If the report risks becoming productivity theater, say so directly.
- Reports may draft/prepare; they must not perform public or financial actions.
