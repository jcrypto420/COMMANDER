# Bad Boys — `Collateral Damage` Motion Fix

**Status:** production-ready edit specification; no posting, paid generation, account action, or public release authorized.

## The locked gag

A farmer **mogs** a banker by using a giant carrot as collateral for a loan. The banker gives him a pathetic tiny coin, and the farmer then uses **his own collateral** to wreck the banker’s desk. That is the title punchline: **Collateral Damage.**

The farmer is in control throughout. The banker is never the action hero and never gets a clever recovery.

## Why the prior generation failed

The prior Kling attempt asked one image-to-video pass to preserve two illustrated characters, faces, clothing, a giant prop, a desk, collision physics, debris, an airborne banker, papers, and a locked camera. That is an impossible collision-simulation request for the model—not a failure of the premise.

**Permanent production rule:** do not use generative video for the swing, impact, destruction, coin handoff, character walk, or any face motion.

## Final 12–15 second motion cut

| Time | Locked panel | Story beat | Motion method |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | P1: banker desk / carrot appears | Giant carrot is presented as collateral. | 2D carrot-tip slide only; banker frozen. |

| 3.7–4.0 | P3: carrot swung | Farmer takes the collateral back and is about to use it. | Hold 6–8 frames. |

| 6.2–8.7 | P5: coin payment | Banker still pays the hilariously tiny approved loan. | 2D coin sprite falls into farmer’s palm and bounces once; characters frozen. |
| 8.7–11.5 | P6: farmer exits | Farmer leaves with the coin; banker remains with the broken desk. | Slow crop/drift; no AI walk cycle. |
| 11.5–13.0 | P6 or black | Title lands after exit. | `COLLATERAL DAMAGE.` |

## Face and style lock

The supplied painted panels are storyboard/reference art only. Final output must use canonical Bad Boys face assets composited or rigged over every character shot; never ask an image-to-video model to redraw a face.

Keep the staging flat, restrained, and deadpan. The joke is the farmer’s calm ownership of an absurd transaction—not chaos for its own sake.

## Optional AI-video use

Only background-only dust settling after the static edit is reviewed. Character identity and all action remain locked in the 2D cut.
