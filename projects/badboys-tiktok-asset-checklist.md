# Bad Boys TikTok Asset Checklist v0

Status: draft only. Do not post, upload, schedule, or spend without Josh approval.

## Purpose

Turn the 30-day rollout into a concrete asset-production queue with predictable
file names and creation order.

## Naming convention

Use simple numbered filenames so the work is easy to sort:

- `badboys-01-manifesto.png`
- `badboys-02-identity.png`
- `badboys-03-joycat.png`
- `badboys-04-philosophy.png`
- `badboys-05-pfp-sticker-pack.png`
- `badboys-06-lore.png`
- `badboys-07-poll.png`

Continue the same pattern through day 30.

## Production order

Make assets in this order:

1. Week 1 foundation art
2. Week 2 character / mascot art
3. Week 3 product / mockup art
4. Week 4 world-building / conversion art
5. Final recap / comparison cards
