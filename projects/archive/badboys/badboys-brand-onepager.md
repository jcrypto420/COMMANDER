# Bad Boys / Joycat / Mog Brand One-Pager v0

Status: draft only. Do not post, publish, upload, or spend without Josh approval.

## Core thesis

Bad Boys is a pseudonymous, meme-native IP lane built around pro-social rebellion:
being a little bad, loud, funny, and unbothered **for good reasons**.

The brand should feel like:

- mischievous, not malicious;
- confident, not cruel;
- controversial, not hateful;
- absurd, not incoherent;
- sales-aware, not scammy;
- CT-native, but bigger than crypto.

## Core phrases

- Moggers Mog.
- Be Bad For Da Good of Humanity.
- Stay joycat.
- Bad for good.
- Good-hearted menace.
- Win loud. Help louder.
- The world needs better bad boys.

## Positioning

Bad Boys / Joycat / Mog is not “another token account” and should not act like one.
It is a character/IP/content engine that can move across:

- memes and short-form social,
- PFP/sticker/wallpaper packs,
- Roblox/game asset concepts,
- merch,
- comics/lore,
- eventual game experiences.

The first business goal is followers + sales, not a full game build.

## Voice

Use:

- short punchy lines;
- repeated maxims;
- playful confrontation;
- absurd confidence;
- direct address;
- meme cadence;
- intentional misspellings only when they fit the bit.

Avoid:

- investment advice;
- token price talk;
- “buy now” token shilling;
- body-shaming;
- alpha/incel framing;
- cruelty;
- hate;
- explicit sexual content;
- racism/misogyny;
- implying affiliation with Mog Coin, Joycat, Pit Viper, or Moggles.

## Character archetype

Working archetype until Josh provides assets:

A chaotic good mascot/crew that acts bad to fight worse things: apathy,
ugliness, scams, boredom, weakness, doomscrolling, fake seriousness, and bad vibes.

Traits:

- unbothered joy;
- comic menace;
- helps people but refuses to be normal about it;
- laughs at fear;
- points at the viewer;
- turns confidence into action.

## Visual direction placeholders

Do not use official Mog/Joycat art or Pit Viper marks/designs.

Original directions to test:

1. Joy-creature mascot: catlike or goblin-cat energy, original silhouette.
2. Sticker-bomb CT look: bold outlines, neon accents, reaction-face energy.
3. “Bad for good” comic panels: mascot doing mischievous good deeds.
4. PFP remix system: original shades/props/stickers, not Moggles/Pit Viper copies.
5. Roblox-friendly simplification: readable character shapes, props, badges.

## First audience promise

Follow Bad Boys if you want daily proof that good can be louder, funnier, and more dangerous than evil.

## First product hypothesis

Start with a low-cost digital pack:

- PFP/banner/wallpaper set,
- sticker/reaction pack,
- printable/poster phrases,
- Roblox/game badge/icon concepts.

Validate demand before merch production or game build.
