# Bad Boys Product Backlog v0

Status: internal draft. Do not spend, launch, sell, list products, create accounts, or contact vendors without Josh approval.

## Goal

Turn Bad Boys / Joycat / Mog from content into revenue experiments without waiting for a perfect brand buildout.

## Product thesis

Start with low-cost signal products that reveal what people actually want:

1. digital identity objects
2. cheap physical objects
3. game/world objects
4. lore/media objects

Do not build expensive inventory first. Let comments, saves, follows, and DMs pick the lane.

## First revenue experiment recommendation

Do not sell first. Poll first.

Week 1 asks the audience what they would actually use. The first monetizable asset should be whichever receives the strongest signal:

- PFP/sticker comments -> make free/paid sticker pack
- tee comments -> mockup + preorder/waitlist
- Roblox comments -> badge/icon pack and simple game concept
- Joycat comments -> lore/comic strip and character merch test

## Definition of signal

A lane is worth building if it gets any of:

- comments that name the product directly
- saves/shares above other posts
- people asking where to get it
- follower bump after product/lore post
- repeat comments on the same character/slogan

## Kill rules

Kill or pause a lane if:

- nobody comments on it after 2–3 tests
- it depends on paid tooling before demand exists
- it risks trademark confusion
- it requires Josh personal-name exposure
