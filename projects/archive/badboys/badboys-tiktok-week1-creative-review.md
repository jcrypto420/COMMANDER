# Bad Boys TikTok Week 1 Creative Review v0

Status: internal review. Do not post, upload, schedule, or spend without Josh approval.

Review date: 2026-06-28

## Verdict

Week 1 is postable as a lean $0 signal test, but the strongest move is not to burn all seven assets as-is.

Use Days 1, 2, 4, and 7 as the first public test candidates. Rework Days 3, 5, and 6 before posting if we want stronger audience signal.

## Asset-by-asset read

### Day 1 — `badboys-01-manifesto.png`

Verdict: keep.

Why it works:
- clean launch statement
- readable on mobile
- strong red/black brand frame
- safe: no finance/token/personal-name claims

Weakness:
- face mark is a little low-contrast inside the red circle; acceptable for launch, better if brightened later.

### Day 2 — `badboys-02-identity.png`

Verdict: keep.

Why it works:
- simple and readable
- good identity statement
- safe and low-risk

Weakness:
- same face-mark contrast issue; it feels more like a title card than a character/world asset.

### Day 3 — `badboys-03-joycat.png`

Verdict: rework before posting if possible.

Why:
- readable and safe
- but it does not show an actual Joycat, so the asset under-delivers on the promise of the caption
- the word `JOYCAT` is strong, but the visual is too abstract for a character intro

Fix direction:
- replace with actual Joycat/companion crop or a simple character silhouette
- if no character art is ready, move this to later and use a philosophy/product poll card first.

### Day 4 — `badboys-04-philosophy.png`

Verdict: keep.

Why it works:
- strongest line in the batch: `You can be kind without being soft.`
- brand-safe pro-social rebellion
- readable and useful as a pinned/evergreen identity post

Weakness:
- could use a more distinctive visual later, but it is good enough now.

### Day 5 — `badboys-05-pfp-sticker-pack.png`

Verdict: rework before posting if possible.

Why:
- the CTA is useful, but the three boxes are placeholders, not product visuals
- asking whether people would run a PFP/sticker pack works better when they can see the actual PFP/sticker/tee concepts

Fix direction:
- show the face mark in a PFP circle, a sticker mockup, and a tee mockup
- make the choice obvious: A=PFP, B=Sticker, C=Tee.

### Day 6 — `badboys-06-lore.png`

Verdict: rework or push later.

Why:
- the line is strong
- but `BB`, `JY`, `4G`, `?` is too cryptic for cold viewers
- the Roblox/comics/toys CTA is good, but the image does not show those lanes visually

Fix direction:
- show three labeled worlds: ROBLOX BADGE, COMIC PANEL, TOY TAG
- keep the main line: `Good guys need better lore.`

### Day 7 — `badboys-07-poll.png`

Verdict: keep, but use after at least one product/mockup visual.

Why it works:
- readable
- clear signal test
- safe

Weakness:
- better if A/B/C cards include small visual examples instead of letters only.
