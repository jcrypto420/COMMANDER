## What “mogging” should mean for Bad Boys

Not alpha-bro body comparison. Not token shilling. Not motivational slogans.

For this brand, mogging = an object has enough visual/status force that the viewer wants to steal it, wear it, save it, or argue with it.

The account should not sound like a startup, creator, or inspirational movement. It should feel like a leak from a weird brand lab: stickers, labels, artifacts, failed products, character evidence, and blunt tests.

## Why previous hooks felt corny

They explained the brand instead of letting the artifact dominate.

Corny patterns to ban:
- “we are…” declarations
- heroic rebellion
- vague big claims
- “for humanity” repeated as a slogan instead of used sparingly
- inspirational moralizing
- anything that sounds like a LinkedIn founder wearing a leather jacket

## Harder tactic stack

1. Artifact-first
   - Post fake stickers, labels, tags, evidence cards, warning labels, product scraps.
   - Copy should be small and cold.

2. Judgment mechanics
   - Use “keep/kill”, “wear/trash”, “PFP/tee/sticker”, “main/side”, “too clean/too cursed”.
   - Make comments feel like voting in a lab test, not helping a creator.

3. Status without speeches
   - A design should look like it already belongs to a group.
   - Do not ask people to join. Make them ask what it is.

4. Controlled disrespect
   - Punch at blandness, fake hype, overexplaining, and soft brands.
   - Do not punch at protected classes, bodies, private people, or financial outsiders.

5. Repetition as bit
   - Same structure, new artifact.
   - “FIELD TEST 001”, “KEEP/KILL”, “DROP CANDIDATE”, “FOUND OBJECT”, “BAD SAMPLE”.

6. Low-context captions
   - Captions should be almost too short.
   - Let comments do the explaining.

## New no-corn direction

Replace “hooks” with labels:
- FIELD TEST 001
- KEEP / KILL
- DROP CANDIDATE
- FOUND OBJECT
- BAD SAMPLE
- DO NOT EXPLAIN THIS
- MAIN OR SIDE QUEST
- TOO CLEAN / TOO CURSED
- WOULD YOU WEAR IT?
- STEAL THIS FORMAT
