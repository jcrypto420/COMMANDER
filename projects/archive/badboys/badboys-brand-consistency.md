# Bad Boys Brand Consistency v0

Status: draft only. Do not post, publish, upload, or spend without Josh approval.

## Source of truth

The strategist package says the OG face logo geometry is the source of truth.
Use the face as a symbol first, then build everything else around it.

## Core brand rule

Be Bad For the Good of Humanity.

Meaning:
- defiant optimism
- creative mischief
- abundance / regeneration
- playful rebellion
- never dull, cynical, or obedient to boring systems

## What the brand should feel like

- mischievous, not malicious
- confident, not cruel
- rebellious, not random
- optimistic, not corny
- underground, not sloppy
- funny, but still coherent

## Logo / mark rules

Use:
- the OG face mark as the primary identity
- subtle chest hits, patches, pins, stickers, embroidery, woven labels, sleeve marks
- hidden easter eggs in larger artwork
- exact face geometry when the mark is shown publicly

Do not:
- redraw the face loosely
- soften the eyebrow angles
- change the smirk into a generic smile
- substitute random human faces or hats
- let AI make “close enough” versions

## Joycat rule

Joycat is a companion force, not a disposable sidekick.
It can travel with BAD BOYS, but should not be swallowed by the core brand.

Best use:
- crossover scenes
- comic storytelling
- companion accessories
- humor / mogging energy

## Visual system

Core identity:
- clean face geometry
- instantly recognizable silhouette
- stable proportions
- strong attitude

Supporting world:
- vintage comic energy
- psychedelic poster texture
- distressed print marks
- stickers
- hidden symbols
- collectible / character-world layering

## Public safety / IP guardrails

- No copied third-party marks.
- No unlicensed celebrity or artist likenesses.
- No protected brand lookalikes in commercial products.
- `badbraums.png` needs extra review before any public use.
- Treat all assets as internal reference unless Josh approves public use.

## Approved use categories

- apparel graphics
- stickers / patches / pins
- Roblox iconography and UGC branding
- comics / lore panels
- product packaging
- hidden easter eggs
- 3D mascot/avatar references, if the face attitude is preserved

## Draft voice

Use short punchy lines.
Repeat maxims.
Keep the bit strong and the intent clear.

Good examples:
- Moggers Mog.
- Be Bad For Da Good of Humanity.
- Stay joycat.
- Win loud. Help louder.
- Good-hearted menace.

Bad examples:
- token shilling
- cruelty
- explicit content
- hate
- body-shaming
- generic corporate brand language
