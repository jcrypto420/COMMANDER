## Decision rules

Double down when:
- a post gets comments with product/lore requests
- saves or shares stand out vs other posts
- a slogan gets repeated back by viewers
- profile visits/follows increase after a specific lane

Change direction when:
- a hook feels cringe/corny after review
- commenters misunderstand the brand
- a post gets no signal after 2–3 variations
- a visual looks placeholder-heavy
