## Fall offer v0 — "Native Prairie Patch Install"

One bounded package, sized to solo capacity (1–2/week). Not a menu — a single
concrete yes/no offer:

- **What:** fall site prep (chop/drop mulching, weed suppression) + winter
  planting of Oklahoma native perennials/grasses in one defined patch
  (target: a 200–500 sq ft residential bed — small enough to solo in a day
  or two, big enough to look intentional, not a garden-center afterthought).
- **Why fall:** native perennials establish root systems over winter with far
  less water/babysitting than a spring planting — genuinely the right season,
  not just "when Josh has time."
- **Who it's for:** OKC-metro homeowners who want a native/pollinator patch
  but don't want to source plants, plan spacing, or do the labor themselves.
- **Deliverable:** site walk + a simple planting plan, prepped bed, plants
  installed, one care sheet (watering first 6 weeks, what NOT to do over
  winter).
- **Price:** **[JOSH INPUT NEEDED]** — cannot responsibly invent a number
  without your real plant-cost basis and target hourly rate. Frame for the
  one-pager once you give a number: a flat "site prep + install" price for a
  defined sq-ft band, plants billed separately at cost + margin, or a single
  all-in price per patch size tier. Pick one model and one number and this
  becomes a real quote sheet in five minutes.
- **Capacity guardrail:** 1–2 installs/week, fall only. The offer doesn't
  promise a start date beyond "your slot in the fall queue" until 3–5 real
  intake responses exist — no overselling a one-person operation.

## Lead channels (real, verified 2026-07-06 — not invented contacts)

No outreach yet — these are the channels to work once Josh approves, ranked
by effort:

1. **Oklahoma Native Plant Society (ONPS)** — active OKC-area chapter,
   meetings + publications. Free, high-trust audience already sold on native
   plants; the easiest "warm" room to be in. [oknativeplants.org](https://oknativeplants.org/)
2. **Oklahoma Native Plant Network (ONPN) service directory** — a real
   vendor/service listing Primoscapes could apply to join, putting it next to
   the plant nurseries people already search for.
   [onpn.org/service-directory](https://www.onpn.org/service-directory)
3. **Blue Thumb (Oklahoma Conservation Commission)** — state stream-protection
   program that actively promotes native/eco-functional landscaping and runs
   a "Sustainable Landcare Training." A completed training could be a real,
   verifiable credibility line on the one-pager (not marketing fluff) and
   Blue Thumb itself is a plausible referral source for homeowners asking
   "who actually does this." [bluethumbok.com](https://www.bluethumbok.com/)
4. **Existing OKC native-landscaping businesses** (Blue House Urban Farm, CLS
   and Associates, Oklahoma Wildscapes, Pollinators + Prairies, Sustainable
   Gardening Solutions, Sweetleaf Landscaping) — not competitors to fear at
   1–2 jobs/week; realistic overflow-referral partners once they're too busy
   for a small residential patch. Also useful as pricing/positioning
   reference before Josh sets a number above.
5. **Low-cost local channels:** Nextdoor (OKC-metro neighborhoods), local
   gardening/native-plant Facebook groups — free, direct-to-homeowner, but
   noisier than the above.
