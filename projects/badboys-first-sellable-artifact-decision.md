# BB-24 — First Sellable Artifact Decision

**Status:** internal decision packet — no storefront, account, listing, upload, sale, or spend authorized
**Decision date:** 2026-07-11
**Recommendation:** bank a **three-piece die-cut sticker sheet**; do not open a store until one Bad Boys content format earns a clear keep signal.

## The product call

### Pick: `Bad Boys Mark Sheet v0`

A small physical sticker sheet is the first product-shaped artifact—not a PFP download, not a tee, and not a broad merch catalog.

| Piece | Existing source | Treatment | Why it belongs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canonical face die-cut | `assets/badboys/INSIDEFACE NOBG.png` | Preserve the canonical double-outline, asymmetric eyes/brows, and smirk exactly; transparent die-cut boundary only | The real, recognizable mark—not new generated art |
| Bone-circle face die-cut | `assets/badboys/account-ready-real-assets-v0/avatar-background-test/white-sticker.png` | Use only after a final crop/edge cleanup check | Existing real-asset variant; visibly readable at avatar size |
| Small wordless mark | Canonical face source, reduced/one-color treatment only | No slogan copy, no new character, no AI-generated variation | Makes the sheet feel like a restrained object rather than merch copywriting |

**What it is not:** a six-slogan bundle, a generative art pack, a token/NFT, or a launch campaign. The older “Good With Teeth” concept is useful historical context, but its text-heavy version is not the current product direction.

## Why this beats the alternatives

| Option | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Three-piece die-cut sticker sheet** | **Pick** | Lowest production complexity; uses existing recognizable visual assets; physical enough to test willingness to buy without a clothing-size/return problem. |
| PFP/download pack | Hold | Easy to make but weak as a paid object and can look like another generic crypto/freebie pack. Keep as a later bundle bonus, not the first sale. |
| Tee/chest badge | Defer | Needs apparel mockups, sizing, print-quality validation, fulfillment choices, and more taste risk before audience signal exists. |

## Storefront path — research only, no account created

1. **Fourthwall — future first choice after signal.** Its published pricing says no monthly fees, no upfront costs, and no contracts; it says it acts as merchant of record and handles payment processing. That is the cleanest low-overhead print-on-demand path *once Josh approves opening an account.* Source: [Fourthwall pricing](https://fourthwall.com/pricing).
2. **Etsy — second choice if marketplace discovery becomes the goal.** It publishes a $0.20 listing fee plus transaction/payment-processing fees and says a one-time shop setup fee may apply. More marketplace exposure, but more generic-shop/fee surface. Source: [Etsy sell](https://www.etsy.com/sell).
3. **Shopify — defer.** Its current Basic plan page shows $29/month on annual billing after a short promotion. That fixed cost is unnecessary before product-market signal. Source: [Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing).

## Signal gate

**Do not create a storefront yet.** Open a Fourthwall account only after one of these occurs:

- A Bad Boys short is published by Josh and earns a concrete keep/kill response that supports the mark or format; or
- Josh explicitly wants the product to be the test and approves account creation plus any resulting fees/tax/onboarding surface.

## Local production brief (safe to execute after Josh says make the sheet)

- Create print-ready transparent PNG masters at 2000px minimum from the canonical source; do not redraw or AI-generate the face.
- Create one static preview sheet using only the three chosen marks.
- Check edge quality, contrast, and legibility at small-phone size before any vendor upload.
- Keep every output local and unlisted until Josh explicitly approves a storefront and listing.
