## Mechanism taxonomy (plain language)

- **fiat custodial** — Issuer holds cash and short-dated Treasuries with custodians; redemption is direct with the issuer, KYC-gated. The live risks are custodial, banking-rail, and attestation quality — not market structure.
- **hybrid CDP + RWA** — Overcollateralized crypto vaults plus substantial real-world-asset and fiat-stablecoin reserves; peg held by PSM-style swap modules while they stay funded.
- **delta-neutral synthetic** — Staked crypto collateral hedged with short perpetual futures. Solvency rides on funding rates staying tolerable and hedging venues staying open; mint/redeem runs through whitelisted market makers.
- **issuer-managed crypto** — Issuer-managed crypto collateral pool; reserve reporting is issuer-published rather than independently attested in our sources.
- **CDP (immutable)** — Liquity-style immutable contracts: ETH/LST collateral, user-set rates, a stability pool absorbs liquidations. No admin keys to misuse, and none to fix anything either.
- **CDP (soft liquidation)** — Curve's LLAMMA design: collateral converts to stablecoin gradually across a price band instead of one-shot liquidation — gentler in chop, still exposed to gap moves.
- **CDP (hard liquidation)** — Minted against surplus crypto collateral; solvency defended by one-shot liquidations when positions breach their threshold.
- **leveraged split** — Collateral exposure split into a stable tranche and a leveraged tranche (f(x) design); the leveraged side absorbs volatility for as long as someone wants to hold it.

## What would make each less boring

- **USD1:** attestation cadence slipping or reserves drifting from T-bills.
- **RLUSD:** attestation gaps or concentration in redemption rails.
- **USDT:** a delayed or qualified reserve attestation.
- **USDG:** attestation gaps or redemption friction across partner venues.
- **PYUSD:** attestation gaps or issuer policy changes to freeze/redeem rules.
- **USDC:** attestation gaps or a repeat of banking-partner contagion.
- **DAI:** RWA counterparty impairment or PSM buffer depletion.
- **USDS:** RWA counterparty impairment or governance-driven collateral drift.
- **USDe:** sustained negative funding or a hedging-venue failure.
- **USDD:** already less boring: no independent attestation of the collateral pool in our sources.
- **BOLD:** stability-pool depletion during an LST depeg.
- **crvUSD:** a collateral crash faster than LLAMMA's band can rebalance.
- **GHO:** a liquidation cascade on Aave or a facilitator misconfiguration.
- **fxUSD:** leveraged-tranche demand evaporating, leaving the stable side unbuffered.

## Score method, v1.1

- **Peg stability (0–30):** worst deviation from $1 across available price sources (DefiLlama, CoinGecko), scaled linearly at 10 points per 100 bps. Assets priced by only one source are marked n/a in the other column — the fallback is never silent.
- **Solvency model (0–25) and redemption clarity (0–15):** assigned per mechanism class from the published matrix below — not from DefiLlama's binary fiat/crypto tag. Changing the matrix is a methodology change and requires a gate.
- **Concentration / custody structure (0–15):** inverse of the largest chain's share of circulating supply in the DefiLlama snapshot.
- **Incident history (0–15):** neutral 15 until the incident ledger exists; no incidents are model-invented.
- **Asset-class filter:** tokenized cash-equivalent funds (BUIDL, USYC, USDY) are classified by known fund identity and excluded from peg scoring.
