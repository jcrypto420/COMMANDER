## Score method, v1 interpretation
- **Peg stability (0–30):** worst of DefiLlama vs CoinGecko deviation from $1, scaled linearly at 10 points per 100 bps.
- **Reserve / mechanism quality (0–25):** fiat-backed > crypto-backed > algorithmic/other, based on `pegMechanism` in the snapshot.
- **Redemption clarity (0–15):** same snapshot-derived mechanism proxy, kept separate to preserve the PRD's draft rubric structure.
- **Concentration / custody structure (0–15):** inverse of the largest chain's share of circulating supply in the DefiLlama snapshot.
- **Incident history (0–15):** neutral 15 in v0 until an incident ledger is added; no incidents are model-invented.
- **Asset-class filter:** tokenized cash-equivalent funds (BUIDL, USYC, USDY) are classified by known fund identity and excluded from peg scoring — see the table above.
