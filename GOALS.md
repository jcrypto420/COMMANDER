cost low. Build the smallest durable system that runs Hermes, executes safe
bounded tasks, and advances Josh's money-making projects every day.

- Pi inventory captured (`scripts/pi_inventory.sh`).

- Stronger local inference offloaded to a Mac mini / custom PC; Pi stays the
  orchestrator.
