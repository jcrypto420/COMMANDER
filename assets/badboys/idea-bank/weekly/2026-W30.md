## 3-5 script-card drafts
- title: wire status pending
  - target: institutional finance bureaucracy
  - beats:
    1. Two banker Bad Boys stand in front of a giant monitor that reads `WIRE STATUS: PENDING`.
    2. One banker sips coffee while the other stares at the screen.
    3. Narrator, flat and reassuring: “Your funds are moving.”
    4. The screen never changes.
    5. Narrator: “They have always been moving.”
  - cast / asset variant: pinstripe banker duo
  - voice mode: narrator
  - loop note: end on the unchanged monitor so the first frame can repeat cleanly
  - max length: ~18s

- title: content.
  - target: algorithm culture
  - beats:
    1. Plain-face mascot holds a sign that says `content`.
    2. Narrator: “This is content.”
    3. Slow zoom; nothing else happens.
    4. Narrator: “Do not like it. Do not follow it. It changes nothing.”
    5. Mascot keeps the same smirk.
  - cast / asset variant: plain face
  - voice mode: narrator
  - loop note: mirror the start frame at the end frame
  - max length: ~20s

- title: risk management.
  - target: trader brain / fake prudence
  - beats:
    1. Cowboy Bad Boy watches a line chart drop off the wall.
    2. He calmly places a second cowboy hat on top of the first.
    3. Silence.
    4. Another tiny chart falls.
    5. He adds a third hat without blinking.
  - cast / asset variant: cowboy
  - voice mode: silent
  - loop note: each hat addition is the beat; no dialogue needed
  - max length: ~12s

- title: day 1.
  - target: hustle culture
  - beats:
    1. Plain-face mascot lies on the floor, perfectly still.
    2. Narrator starts a fake grind countdown: “5am: rise. 5:01: grind. 5:02: cold plunge.”
    3. The mascot never moves.
    4. Narrator keeps going: “5:03: gratitude. 5:04: dominate.”
    5. Caption: `day 1.`
  - cast / asset variant: plain face
  - voice mode: narrator
  - loop note: start on the floor pose and end on the same pose
  - max length: ~25s

- title: compliance theater.
  - target: corporate absurdity
  - beats:
    1. Chef variant sits at a blank desk with a stack of empty forms.
    2. He stamps the top page once.
    3. He stamps the next page.
    4. The stack never gets smaller.
    5. A final stamp lands with no effect.
  - cast / asset variant: chef
  - voice mode: silent or single flat narrator if needed
  - loop note: the stack should look identical before and after
  - max length: ~15s

## Corny-detector notes

- Avoid any “wait for it” energy, moral lesson, or motivational turn in the script cards.
